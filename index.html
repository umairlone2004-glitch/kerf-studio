02 — Process Roughly 7 weeks, start to handoff

Short cycles.
Loud critique.
No theatre.

We do not run discovery for three months. We find the expensive problem in week one, put something real in front of users by week three, and ship the system by week seven.

Working with us: one designer lead, one writer, weekly demos on a live prototype — not a deck.

    Diagnose

    We read your funnel, watch five sessions, and interview the two people who close deals. The output is a short list of screens that are actually costing money.

05 — Questions Answers before the call
Who actually does the work?

The two people you meet on the call. We stay deliberately small — six designers and one writer — and we do not subcontract. If a project needs a skill we do not have, we say so and recommend someone who does.

Do you write code?

We build interactive HTML/CSS prototypes your team can click, and we work inside your component library when you have one. Production code stays with your engineers — we join their review process instead.

How fast can you start?

Usually two to four weeks out. We take one new engagement at a time, which is why there is a single slot on the calendar rather than a queue.

What do you need from us?

Access to analytics, five recent support threads, one person who can make decisions in a day, and a developer available for a 30-minute weekly review. That is genuinely the whole list.

Do you work with early-stage teams?

Yes, mostly pre-seed to Series C. For very early teams we prefer the sprint format — it keeps the commitment small while the product is still finding its shape.

What happens after handoff?

Two weeks of free design support while your engineers build, then a paid retainer if you want us in the room for the next cycle. No lock-in, no seat licences.
